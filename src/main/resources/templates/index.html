Rules & Payout Terms
  • In order to purchase a square, player must pay the full value of the square.
  • Players can buy an unlimited number of squares per board.
  • Once a game has started, the board will be locked and no additional squares can be purchased.
  • To view your squares please choose the game with the selected dollar amount. Your squares will be highlighted in green.
  • After all squares within a grid are sold, the numbers are randomly generated to determine the value of each square.
  • When selecting a square, look at the board number you are selecting from. If a matchup has more than one (1) full board, new boards will be created appear with a different board number.
  • Once purchased, a square cannot be refunded or exchanged.
  • Once a square selected, it is reserved for 30 seconds, until the player release or taken once the player has purchased it.
  • Players whose square numbers match the last digit of the score of each team at the end of each period are the winners.
  • Square pools graded are based on the end of the score of each said period.
  • Prizes for sporting events with four quarters are split as follows: 1st Quarter – 12.5%, Half-time – 25%, 3rd Quarter – 12.5%, End of Game – 50%. 10% of this total will be deducted by the house.
  • Prizes for sporting events with two halves are split as follows: 1st Half – 33.33%, Full Game – 66.66%. 10% of this total will be deducted by the house.
  • It is possible for a square to win multiple prizes in the same game.
  • Prizes are paid out in real money to winners account balance.
  • Prizes do not have a rollover, however any existing rollover must be met before a withdrawal can be requested.
  • A board is only valid for the game named in said board. There may be multiple boards per game.
  • At least 35% of the board must be purchased in order for the board to have action.
  • If 35% of the board is not purchased, all buy-ins will be refunded.
  • In sporting events with two halves in case there are no winners on the 1st Half, the prize money will be added to the End of Game prize. If there is no winner for the End of Game, that prize (and any accumulated prize money) will be refunded equally into accounts minus the 10% rake.
  • In sporting events with four quarters in case there are no winners on the 1st Quarter, the prize money will be added to the 1st Half prize. If there is no winner for the 1st Half, the prize money will be added (and any accumulated prize money) to the 3rd Quarter prize. If there is no winner for the 3rd Quarter, the prize money will be added (and any accumulated prize money) to the End of Game prize. If there is no winner for the End Of Game, that prize (and any accumulated prize money) will be refunded equally into accounts minus the 10% rake.
  • The team on the left-hand side of the board (vertical) is the away team while the team at the top of the board (horizontal) is the home team.
  • Boards are visible for up to 24 hours after the event has ended.
  • The house reserves the right to void any purchase that violates platform rules or local regulations. Participants must be of legal age to play. By purchasing a square, you agree to these terms and the posted payout structure.
