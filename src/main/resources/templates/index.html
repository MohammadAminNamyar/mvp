Terms and Conditions

Squares games are based on a 10x10 grid with random digits assigned after the board locks. Purchased squares are final, and payouts are based on the last digit of each team’s score at the end of each quarter. Please review the house percentage and minimum squares required to activate each board before purchasing.

The house reserves the right to void any purchase that violates platform rules or local regulations. Participants must be of legal age to play. By purchasing a square, you agree to these terms and the posted payout structure.
