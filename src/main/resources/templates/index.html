Terms and Conditions

By purchasing squares, you agree to the house rules for this Squares Pool. All entries are final once confirmed. Boards lock at kickoff or when filled, and digits are revealed after lock. Payouts are based on confirmed quarter scores and recorded in the admin console. If a game is postponed or canceled, the board will be reset or refunded at the administrator's discretion.

Participants must be 18 years or older and agree to play responsibly. The house reserve and minimum squares required to activate a board are shown on each board view.
